Integration role:
- The cache submits reads/writes through sched_submit.
- The scheduler is the sole caller of disk_raw_read/disk_raw_write in the full layer.
- The lock manager calls wait-for hooks at wait, grant, and release points.

TCP network integration:
- TCP and FIFO requests share the same dispatcher.
- Remote connection identities map to bounded graph session IDs.
- Open, read, write, close, and logout lock routing is centralized in the dispatcher.
